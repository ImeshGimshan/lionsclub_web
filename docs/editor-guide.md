# Editor guide: updating the club website

This guide is for club members who keep the website up to date. You do not need any technical
knowledge. Everything is done in the **Sanity Studio**, a website for editing content.

- **Studio address:** <https://lions-dummalasuriya.sanity.studio>
- **Signing in:** use the Google account or email address you were invited with. Never share a login.

Changes appear on the public website within a few seconds of pressing **Publish**.

---

## The basics

### What the Studio contains

Everything visitors see: club details, homepage wording, projects, photos and officers. Enquiries from
the website don't come here; they arrive on WhatsApp (see [Handling enquiries](#handling-enquiries)).

### Drafts and Publish

- When you change something, the Studio saves a **draft** automatically. Visitors do not see drafts.
- Press **Publish** (bottom right) when you are ready. The website updates within a few seconds.
- Changed your mind? Use the menu next to Publish → **Discard changes**.
- Recent versions are kept. Use the **History** (clock icon) to see or restore an earlier version. How far
  back it goes depends on the club's Sanity plan, so don't rely on it as a backup.

### Before publishing, check

1. Names are spelt exactly as the person wants them.
2. Numbers have been confirmed by the club and you know where they came from.
3. You have permission to publish every photo, especially photos of children or vulnerable people.
4. Nothing promises something the club has not agreed (payments, membership approval, emergency help).

## Common tasks

### Add a new project

**Website content → Projects → + (Create)**

1. **Details tab:** title, service category and a one-line summary. Add partner clubs if it was a joint
   effort (e.g. "Joint programme with eight Lions Clubs").
2. **Dated activities tab:** add one activity for each date the club served. For each:
   - date and place
   - a short description
   - **Headline figure** (optional): the number, what it counts (e.g. "saplings donated") and **where
     the number comes from**. The Studio will not publish a number without a source.
   - **Timeline photo** (optional): only choose a photo you know belongs to that exact date.
3. **Photos & gallery tab:** drag photos in. The **first photo is the cover**. Give each photo a short
   description (alt text) of what is visible, for example "Members planting coconut saplings beside the
   temple." Tick **Publication permission confirmed** once the club has agreed.
4. Press **Publish**.

The activity appears on the timeline in date order, and the photos appear as a gallery album.

> Keep separate events separate. Don't add two activities' numbers together.

### Add photos to an existing project

Open the project → **Photos & gallery** → drag the photos in → add alt text → **Publish**.

### Add a gallery album that isn't a project

For socials, meetings or ceremonies: **Gallery albums → +**. Add a title, category, description and the
photos. **Gallery position** controls the order (lower numbers first; projects and albums share it).

### Update the officers for a new year

1. For each outgoing officer: open them → set **Status** to **Archived** → Publish. Don't delete them;
   the archive is the club's record.
2. **Officers → + (Create)** for each new officer: display name, role, **service year** (format
   `2027/28`), a square portrait, display order (1 = first) and status **Current**.
3. Update the service year in **Club settings** too. It is not changed automatically.

### Change contact details

**Club settings → Contact tab.** The phone number is entered twice: once as it should look
(`+94 76 564 6292`) and once as digits only for the call button (`+94765646292`).

**WhatsApp number for enquiries** is where the website's enquiry form sends messages. Use `+`
followed by digits, no spaces. **Change it whenever the secretary changes**, or new enquiries keep
going to the old secretary's phone.

### Change the homepage wording

**Homepage**, then choose a tab: Hero, Our club, Section headings, Get involved, Support, Ribbon & footer.

- In headings, put words between **asterisks** to show them in the elegant italic style:
  `Service that *means something.*`
- Some lists have fixed sizes because of the page design (for example exactly three joining steps). The
  Studio tells you if a list is too long or too short.

### Change the highlight numbers under the main photo

**Club settings → Homepage tab → Highlights.** Up to three figures, plus the period they cover
(e.g. "July–September 2026"). Only use verified figures.

**Highlights note** (same tab, optional) adds a short line under the figures, for example "These
figures describe selected projects only. They are not lifetime totals." Leave it empty to hide it.

### Change how the site looks in Google and when shared

**Club settings → Search & sharing:** page title, description and the image shown when the link is
shared on Facebook or WhatsApp.

## Handling enquiries

The form in the **Get involved** section opens WhatsApp on the visitor's phone or computer with a
message ready to send to the **WhatsApp number for enquiries** (Club settings → Contact). When they
press Send, it arrives as a normal WhatsApp chat, for example:

> Hello Lions Club of Dummalasuriya,
>
> I'm Nimal from Kuliyapitiya. I'd like to ask about becoming a member.
>
> (Sent from the club website)

Nothing is stored on the website or in the Studio. Reply in the same chat.

**Good practice**

- Reply within a few days, even if only to say when you'll have an answer.
- Only use people's details to reply to their enquiry. Don't add them to groups or broadcast lists
  without asking, and don't share their number.
- If someone asks you to delete their messages, delete the chat.
- When the secretary changes, update the WhatsApp number in the Studio on the same day.

## What you cannot change here (on purpose)

These stay fixed in the website code and need the developer:

- The Lions International mission, vision and "We Serve" motto (official, attributed wording)
- The eight Lions global causes
- The website menu and section order
- The enquiry form's topics and its "How your message is sent" notice (they must match how the form
  actually works)
- The Lions emblem, colours and fonts (Lions brand rules)

## Getting help

If something looks wrong on the website, first check you pressed **Publish**. Then contact the website
developer with: what you changed, when, and a screenshot.
